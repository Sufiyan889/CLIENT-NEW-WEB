Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your next digital project 🚀

How long does it take to build a website?

On average, a standard business website takes 2 to 4 weeks to complete — including design, development, and revisions. Complex projects like eCommerce or AI-based platforms can take up to 6 to 8 weeks.

How much does a custom website cost?

Our website packages start from $499 for basic designs. The final cost depends on complexity, features, and integrations like AI, chatbots, or dashboards. You’ll always receive a clear quote before we begin.

Do you offer custom designs or templates?

We specialize in 100% custom designs tailored to your brand identity. However, we can also use premium templates if you’re on a tight deadline or budget — ensuring both quality and speed.

Can you maintain or update my existing website?

Absolutely! We provide monthly maintenance plans for updates, security patches, content uploads, and design tweaks — keeping your site secure and up-to-date.

Do you use AI in your projects?

Yes! We integrate advanced AI tools like chatbots, content generators, and analytics systems to make your website smarter, faster, and more interactive.

Do you provide post-launch support?

We offer 24/7 technical support and regular performance checks after launch to ensure everything runs smoothly and efficiently — your success is our priority!
